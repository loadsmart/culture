# Tech Lead Role

### What a Tech Lead is

A Tech Lead is a Senior+ engineer who leads a pod or squad end-to-end: they coordinate what the pod builds, how it builds it, and the relationship with the people who use it. A Tech Lead is also a hands-on engineer, spending most of their time building alongside the others. Not all squads need to have an assigned Tech Lead, it is a resource that EMs and Directors can decide to use when it makes sense.

Tech Lead is an ephemeral role, not a position. It is not a level in the Technical or Management tracks of the Career Path. It is a set of responsibilities given to a Senior engineer to amplify their impact, and it can move between people as squads/pods and priorities change.

The Tech Lead is not a people manager. They do not run performance reviews, decide promotions or own hiring decisions.

### Technical excellence

The Tech Lead raises the technical bar of the pod and makes sure it follows Loadsmart's quality standards.

- Support the squad to reach the best technical decision and own the architecture of the systems it maintains.
- Keep code review fast and useful. Decide what should be checked by people, by automation, or by AI agents.
- Make sure the systems have good tests, monitoring, alerting and logging.

### Stakeholders and Product Relationship

The Tech Lead is the squad/pod's main contact with its stakeholders. They partner but do not need a Product Manager in the room to understand a problem, agree on scope or ship a solution.

- Build a direct relationship with the stakeholders. Know who they are, how they work and what they measure.
- Run discovery with them: watch how they work, understand the problem before the solution, and turn it into small, clear pieces of work.
- Keep stakeholders informed: what is coming, what changed, what shipped and what it achieved.
- Make sure RFCs from the pod clearly state the problem and the proposed solution, and follow Loadsmart's RFC format.

### Who owns what

The Tech Lead owns the pod's day to day decisions. The Engineering Manager owns people, and the Product Manager owns product direction across pods.

| Area | Tech Lead | Engineering Manager | Product Manager |
|--|--|--|--|
| Stakeholder relationship | Owns, day to day | Supports | Owns at product level |
| Product strategy and roadmap | Gives input | Gives input | Owns |
| Delivery (Day-to-day priorities, Jira, AI-DLC, metrics) | Co-owns | Owns | Informed |
| Technical decisions and architecture (SLAs, monitoring) | Owns | Supports | Informed |
| People management (Hiring, performance, career growth and etc.) | Gives input | Owns | Gives input |
